# Jack of All — Privacy Policy (template)

Jack of All does not require an account and does not collect email addresses, contacts, location or device identifiers.

**What is processed:** the display name you type and the in-game actions you take are sent to the game server so the room works for your friends. Room data is kept in server memory only and is deleted when the room ends or after a period of inactivity. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties and the app contains no ads or analytics.

**On your device:** your name, last server address and an anonymous rejoin token are stored locally so you can reconnect after a dropped connection. Uninstalling the app removes them.

**Contact:** YOUR-EMAIL@example.com
